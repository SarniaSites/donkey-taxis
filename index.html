Island tours

Guernsey is small, but it changes every few miles. Tell us how long you have and what you’d like to see, and we’ll plan the drive. A few of our favourite stops:

Plan a tour

  • Petit Bot

    Sheltered south-coast bay with a loophole tower from 1778. Sand and rock pools at low tide.

  • La Vallette

    Sunrise over the water. Around 7am in October.

  • St Peter Port

    Breakfast in town with a view out to Herm and Sark.

  • The cliff paths

    Dropped at one end, collected at the other. Dogs are welcome on the paths.
