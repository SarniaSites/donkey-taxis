### Post 4

The sounds of an afternoon spent at Petit Bot

Secluded and sheltered on the south coast, Petit Bot is a great spot for exploring at any tide height.
 At high tide Petit Bot has a pebbled shore, at low tide it turns into a lovely sandy beach. There are lots of coves and rock pools to enjoy. The ever changing shores is one of the most beautiful parts of the coastal beaches, so you’ll find somewhere new every time.
Petit Bot is also home to a tearoom where you can enjoy Guernsey ice cream, drinks and snacks.
At the bay you’ll also find one of the 15 Loophole Towers that were built to defend the island in 1778-9. A free exhibition of its history is available in the tower too!
 You can also arrange kayak hire at the beach from Outdoor Guernsey to explore the beach and coastline even further.

### Post 18

Planning a visit to our Islands with your four-legged friend? Here's everything you need to know about travelling with a dog.
 Dogs are welcome on the cliff paths, at most al fresco dining locations and even some shops and cafes. You can find a range of pet-friendly accommodation across the islands, from campsites to five-star hotels.
 Most of the Islands’ beaches allow dogs to enjoy the space year-round, from 1st May to 30th September there are restrictions, but there is always a beach available for you all to enjoy.
 Pets visiting from the UK, other Channel Islands and the Isle of Man are generally able to visit without a Pet Passport.
 You can bring your pet to Guernsey on the ferry from the UK and France, operated by Brittany Ferries.
 If you are visiting with a guide dog, they will be welcome across the Islands.
For more information and guidance, head to: https://www.visitguernsey.com/.../visiting-with-your-pet/ See less
